ABOUT ME

Building Systems. Developing People. Creating Sustainable Excellence.

My career has been built around one consistent mission: designing management systems that protect people, strengthen brands and create sustainable value.

Throughout more than a decade across manufacturing, hospitality, education, government and food service, I have led initiatives involving Food Safety, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Halal, Occupational Safety & Health and continuous improvement.

Rather than viewing compliance as the destination, I believe strong management systems become strategic business assets that improve operational performance, reduce organisational risk and build stakeholder confidence.
